Revcenter finds candidates for a role. Tell it who you need, in your own words. It looks across LinkedIn, reads
each profile, scores every person against your brief, and puts the best on your **Shortlist**. You send your
client one link that always shows the latest list.

**3 credits for each new person a search finds for you. People you already have are free.** See
[Credits and pricing](/pricing).

## What you can do

- **Start a search in plain English.** "Plant Controller within 30 miles of Camden, NJ, CPA required." Revcenter
  shows you its plan before anything starts.
- **See why each person fits.** Every candidate gets a score and a short reason.
- **Keep one Project per role.** The brief, the searches and the Shortlist stay together.
- **Ask the AI chat.** "Why is the list short?", "Take Dana off the list", "Find more people like these." AI chat
  is free.
- **Share with your client.** One link, always up to date. Nothing reaches your client until you say yes.
