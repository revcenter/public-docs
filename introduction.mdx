Revcenter finds candidates on LinkedIn for a role, scores each one against what you asked for, and keeps the
people who fit on the project's shortlist. You send your client one link that always shows the live list.

**3 credits per profile delivered. People you already have are free.** See [Pricing and credits](/pricing).

## What you need

1. A Revcenter workspace, on a free trial or a plan.
2. To sign in: connect your chat app, run `revcenter auth login`, or make a key under **Settings → Integrations → API**.
