Some requests for companies are about things a company filter can't see: who owns a company, the software it
runs, an award list it is on, a lawsuit, a deal. Revcenter splits the request first. What our data answers is
free. Web research covers the rest, only when it's needed, and only after you press Start.

## What the chat says

Ask for companies in the project chat, in a people thread or a company thread. The reply depends on the request.

| You ask | The chat answers | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Companies our filters cover ("accounting firms in Denver hiring a tax senior") | A company search, as always. | As a company search. |
| A list of names ("Acme Corp, Globex, Initech") | The names matched to companies we hold: "Matched 2 of 3; Initech not found. Add its website to match it." Nothing is guessed. | Free. |
| Something vague ("fast-growing companies in Denver") | One question, with choices to tap. Each choice says whether it comes from our data or needs research. | Free. |
| Something our data can't fully check ("PE-backed accounting firms in Nashville") | What our data answers and what needs research, the companies whose own page or job postings already say it, then a research card. | The free part is free. Research is priced on its card. |

A reply to the last kind reads like this:

> From our data: Nashville. Needs research: PE-backed. 12 companies say so on their own page or job postings, at
> no charge. A fuller list needs research on the web, which keeps a source for every company it adds.

## The research card

The card shows, before anything runs:

- **What it looks for**, in your words.
- **The place.**
- **Its budget**: 15 web searches and 10 pages read at most. It stops at the first limit and keeps what it found.
- **Its credits**: 50, held when you press Start.
- **When they come back**: if it finds no company with a source, every credit comes back.

Nothing runs until you press Start, and the chat never presses it for you. Not now leaves it.

## What a run gives you

Each company comes with the page that names it, the words on that page that show it, and the kind of evidence
(the company's own page, an investor's portfolio page, a news report, a list, a court or agency record). News,
lawsuits and notices always carry a date, and old ones are left out. Each company is matched to a company we
hold, or marked **Not found**: a company that isn't matched is never guessed.

Companies that our pages and postings already show are listed first, free.

## Scan these for hiring

Tick the companies you want and press **Scan these for hiring**. Only ticked companies that matched go to the
company hiring search, as its usual card, so you can add roles before you start it. That search is priced as
any company search.

## Who can use it

Web research is part of a paid plan. On a trial, the chat still splits the request, matches names and asks its
question, and says that research needs a paid plan. Research is being switched on workspace by workspace; ask
support if you don't see the card. Each workspace can run up to 20 research runs, or 1,000 credits of research,
a month.

## From an agent

Agents use the same three steps through the API and MCP:

| Tool | What it does | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `plan_company_request` | The split, the free first pass, the name matches or the question, and whether research may run, with its card. | Does not use credits. |
| `research_company_list` | Starts a run. MCP asks the person first, showing the card; an agent that can't ask gets the proposal to show them. | Holds 50 credits; charged only when it finds at least one company with a source. |
| `get_company_research` | The run's status and its companies, each with its source and whether it matched. | Does not use credits. |

See [Connect an agent](/tools/mcp) for setup.
