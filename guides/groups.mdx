---
title: "Groups"
description: "Watch people or companies and hear when something changes: read your groups, add and remove members, and pause a group from your own tools."
---

A group is a list of people or companies that Revcenter checks for you on a schedule. When a check finds a
change, such as someone turning on Open to work, starting a new job or a company posting new roles, it is
recorded as a change on the group, and the group's owner gets one email about it.

You make a group in the Revcenter app: pick people or companies from your lists, name the group and choose how
often it is checked. From your own tools, through the API or MCP, you can then read your groups, see what
changed, add or remove members, and pause a group. Making, renaming and deleting a group stay in the app.

## What you can do

| You want to | API | MCP | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| See your groups | `GET /groups` | `list_groups` | Free |
| See one group and its members | `GET /groups/{id}` | `get_group` | Free |
| See what changed | `GET /groups/{id}/changes` | `get_group_changes` | Free |
| Add people or companies | `POST /groups/{id}/members` | `add_to_group` | Free now; raises the group's monthly credits |
| Take someone out | `DELETE /groups/{id}/members/{member}` | `remove_from_group` | Free |
| Pause or resume a group | `POST /groups/{id}/pause` | `pause_group` | Free |

You see the groups you made and the ones shared with your workspace. A workspace admin sees every group. Only a
group's owner or an admin can add, remove or pause; a group someone else shared with you is read-only.

## What a group costs

Each check of each member costs 1 credit for a person and 2 credits for a company. A check is free when the
profile was read in the last day, and when it fails or finds the profile gone or private. So the cost depends
on how many members a group has and how often it is checked. The app shows it as one line, for example 50 people
checked weekly is "About 217 credits a month · checks every Monday".

Every group has a monthly cap (2,000 credits unless you change it in the app). When a group reaches its cap it
stops until the 1st of the next month. A paused group is not checked and costs nothing.

## Adding people or companies

Name each one by its Revcenter candidate id, its firm id, or its LinkedIn address:

```json
{ "members": [{ "linkedin_url": "https://www.linkedin.com/in/example" }, { "candidate_id": "3c2b1a09-8f7e-4d6c-9b5a-4e3d2c1b0a99" }] }
```

- A people group takes candidate ids and LinkedIn profile addresses. A company group takes firm ids and LinkedIn
  company addresses.
- Someone Revcenter has never read can be added by their LinkedIn address. They are read at the group's next
  check.
- Anyone already in the group stays as they are, so sending the same list twice changes nothing.
- Anything that can't be added comes back in `not_found` with the reason. The rest are still added.
- A group holds up to 500 members (10 on a trial). A request that would go past that adds no one and says how
  much room is left.

Adding members makes the group's next checks cost more, so the MCP server asks you first. It shows who would be
added and the new monthly estimate, for example: "Add 1 person to Tax seniors to watch? About 221 credits a month,
checks every Monday." Accept adds them; Decline changes nothing. If your tool can't ask you, it hands back a
proposal to approve with `apply_proposal`. Through the API you can see the same preview first with
`preview_action`.

```json
{ "group_id": "8f2c4a1e-6b3d-4c5e-9a7f-1b2c3d4e5f60", "added": 1, "already_in": 0, "not_found": [], "members": 51, "member_limit": 500, "notice": null, "estimate": { "credits_per_month": 221, "credits_per_check": 51, "line": "About 221 credits a month · checks every Monday" }, "next_check_at": "2026-11-16T11:00:00.000Z" }
```

## Reading changes

`get_group_changes` lists what the checks found, newest first. Each change says who it is about, which rule
fired, what it was before and after, and when it was seen:

```json
{ "id": "5c6d7e8f-9a0b-4c1d-8e2f-3a4b5c6d7e8f", "rule": "open_to_work_on", "label": "Open to work turns on", "summary": "Turned on Open to work", "from": false, "to": true, "member": { "name": "Dana Mercer", "title": "Senior Tax Associate", "company": "Garden State Accounting" }, "observed_at": "2026-11-09T11:04:00.000Z", "recorded_at": "2026-11-09T11:04:02.000Z" }
```

To pick up only what is new, keep the `recorded_at` of the newest change you have and send it back as `since`.
A group's first check only records where everyone is, so it finds no changes. The rules a group can watch are:

| Rule | Fires when |
| --- | --- |
| `open_to_work_on` | A person turns on Open to work |
| `job_change` | A person starts at a new employer or gets a new title |
| `location_change` | A person's profile moves to another place |
| `move_ready` | A person reaches the time in a role when people often move |
| `posting_new` | A company posts a new job with one of the group's titles |
| `hiring_surge` | A company posts 5 or more new jobs in 14 days |
| `posting_repost` | A company posts the same role again |

## Removing and pausing

`remove_from_group` takes one member out, named by the member id `get_group` lists or by their candidate or firm
id. They are not checked again, and their past changes stay on the group. `pause_group` with `paused: true`
stops a group's checks and charges until you resume it with `paused: false`.
