The API uses your workspace's **credits**: the same plan and top-ups as the Revcenter app. There is no separate API
balance. A standard search uses about 1,000 credits, so 37,000 credits is roughly 37 standard searches.

## Prices

A search is charged for what it buys: pages of results and the profiles it reads. The price depends on whether
your workspace is on a plan when the search starts.

| Unit | On a plan | On a trial |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One results page (up to 25 people screened) | 25 credits | 50 credits |
| One profile read | 2 credits | 5 credits |
| Company and location lookups | free | free |
| AI screening and scoring | free | free |
| People already in your workspace's database | free | free |
| Pages and profiles served from cache | free | free |

A page is charged in full even when it is the last one and lists fewer than 25 people. A person whose profile
is read once in a search is one read. Contact details (email or phone), where a search asks for them, are charged
as used.

A search keeps the prices it started with: one started on a trial is charged trial prices even if the workspace
moves to a plan before it finishes. **Settings → Plan & billing** shows your workspace's prices, and on a trial
the prices a plan gets.

At the paid prices, 1,000 credits covers about 20 pages (500 people screened) and 240 profile reads, assuming a
search reads about 12 people a page; at trial prices, about 9 pages (225 people) and 108 reads. The search plan in
the app and the launch preview say what your cap covers.

| Action | Uses |
| --- | --- |
| Launching a search | Up to its `max_credits` (by default your workspace's most per search); unused credits are refunded |
| Expanding a search | Up to its `max_credits` (by default your workspace's most per search); unused credits are refunded |
| Database-only search | nothing |
| Rescoring (cut-off, requirements), database pass | nothing |
| Reading, editing and exporting | nothing |

## How a search is charged

1. **Cap.** `launch_search` and `expand_search` take `max_credits`: any whole number from 250 to 20,000, with
   presets of 500, 1,000, 2,500 and 5,000. Leave it out for your workspace's most per search. The CLI flag is
   `--max-credits`. A search
   buys a page of results only when its cap also covers reading the profiles on it, and 250 is one page with
   everyone on it read, so a smaller cap is refused with `400 max_credits_too_low`. In the app, say the cap in
   your request ("only use 2,500 credits", "cap it at 5k credits"); the search plan shows it.
2. **Limit.** Every cap is held to your workspace's most per search. On a trial that is 1,000 credits, whatever
   the request asks. On a plan it is 5,000 unless a workspace admin sets another under **Settings → Workspace →
   Credits per search** (250 to 20,000). A cap is also held to the credits you have left. When a cap was held
   lower, the launch says so in `credits_note`, and `credits_reserved` is the cap the search runs under.
3. **Reserve.** The cap is held from your balance when the search starts and returned as `credits_reserved`. If
   the balance can't cover it, the launch returns `402 shortlist_allocation_exhausted` and nothing runs.
4. **Charge.** A search spends credits as it buys pages of results and reads profiles, and keeps going until it
   has used its cap or LinkedIn has no one left for its titles and places. It buys the next page only when its
   cap still covers that page and the reads a page usually needs. When it finishes it is charged
   pages × page price + reads × read price, never more than the cap, and the rest comes back.
   `GET /v1/searches/{id}` shows `credits_charged` once it has finished (`null` while it runs) and
   `credits_breakdown`, the arithmetic: for example `"10 pages × 25 + 81 reads × 2 = 412 credits"`, with
   `", capped at 1,000"` when the cap held it. `GET /v1/projects/{id}/spend` and `revcenter spend` show the same
   line for every search in a project.
5. **Refund.** A search that fails, or never starts, is refunded in full. So is one whose cap can't buy its
   first page: it stops with `stop_reason` `cap_too_small` instead of reading anyone. A retry with the same `Idempotency-Key`
   never reserves twice.

A search runs until its cap is used or the market runs out, so a bigger cap reads further and usually uses more;
it never costs more than the cap. Use 500 for a quick look at a narrow role and 2,500 or 5,000 for a broad one or
several places.

## Balance

Credits left: **Settings → Usage**, `GET /v1/me/usage` or `revcenter usage` (`credits_remaining`). Credits held by
running searches come back into it as they finish. Plans and top-ups add 1,000 credits for every shortlist on the
price sheet, at your plan's rate.

Without an active plan, requests return `402 plan_required`. Add credits under **Settings → Plan & billing**, or
with `revcenter topup`. Each action's cost is in `GET /v1/actions` and on its
[API reference](/api-reference/introduction) page.
