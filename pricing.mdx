**3 credits per profile delivered. People you already have are free.** Results pages are free too.

You pay for one thing: new people. Each time a search brings a person into your workspace for the first time, it
costs 3 credits, whatever their score. After that, every later search can use that person again for free.

## What a shortlist costs

You pay for the new people a search looked at, not for the people who made the Shortlist. A search reads
everyone who might fit and scores them. The best make your Shortlist. Each new person read costs 3 credits,
whether they made the list or not. People a search skips before reading cost nothing.

For example, a search that delivers 81 new people is charged 243 credits (81 × 3), whether 20 or 60 of them make the
Shortlist. Searching the same role again later costs less, because the people you already have are free.

Before a search starts, Revcenter shows the most it can use and what that covers, for example "1,000 credits
covers up to 333 profiles". When it finishes, the project shows what it cost and why, for example
"81 profiles × 3 = 243 credits".

## How a search's credit limit works

1. **Set aside.** When a search starts, its credit limit is set aside from your balance. On a plan the limit is
   5,000 credits, unless your admins choose another under **Settings → Workspace → Credits per search**. On a
   trial it is 1,000. You can always ask for less: "only use 2,500 credits".
2. **Charged.** When the search finishes, it is charged 3 credits for each new person, never more than the limit.
3. **Returned.** Whatever it didn't use comes back to your balance. A search that fails or never starts gives
   everything back.

A bigger limit lets a search look further. It never costs more than the limit, and it stops early when few
people match. **Search my database again** uses the same search's limit while the search still holds it. Once a
search has finished, use **Expand** instead.

A project's searches can hold up to three searches' worth of credits at once. For a different role or place,
start a new project.

## Company searches

You can also search for companies that are hiring. You pay per company, for the information you choose:

- **Companies hiring now:** 2 credits per company. Each comes with its open roles, size, industry and a hiring
  score.
- **Hiring signals:** 5 more credits per company. Senior leaders who left or joined, title changes and a new top
  leader, with dates.
- **Decision makers:** 3 more credits per company. Who to call: up to 6 named people.

A company list that finds anything costs at least 100 credits. Hiring signals and decision makers are only
charged for companies where something turns up. Companies already in your results, and staffing agencies left
out, are free. Trials pay the same prices.

Before you start, Revcenter shows what your credits cover, for example:

> Up to 1,000 credits: up to 100 companies, the top 50 with hiring signals and decision makers.

When the search finishes, the project shows where the credits went, for example
"Finding 60 · Hiring signals 150 · Decision makers 90".

## Free trial

A free trial gives you 500 credits at plan prices: the same 3 credits per new person. When the trial credits are
used up, choose a plan to keep searching.

## Your balance

Your credits are under **Settings → Usage**. Credits set aside by searches still running come back as they
finish. A plan adds its credits each period. If you need more before then, buy a top-up under
**Settings → Plan & billing**, at your plan's own rate.
