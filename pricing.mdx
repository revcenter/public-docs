**3 credits per profile delivered. People you already have are free.**

A search is charged for each new person it delivers to your workspace, whatever their score. Once your
workspace has someone, every later search and the candidate database use them again for free.

## What is free

- People your workspace already has
- Results pages, company and place lookups, AI screening and scoring
- Re-scoring: a new cut-off or new requirements for everyone already found
- Reading, projects, shortlist changes, sharing the list with your client, and exports

## How a search's cap works

1. **Reserved.** When a search starts, its cap is held from your credits. The cap is your workspace's limit per
   search unless you name less ("only use 2,500 credits"). On a plan the limit is 5,000 unless your admins set
   another under **Settings → Workspace → Credits per search**.
2. **Charged.** When it finishes, it is charged 3 credits for each new profile it delivered, never more than the
   cap. A search that found 81 new people is charged 243 credits; 1,000 credits covers up to 333 profiles.
3. **Refunded.** The rest of the cap comes back. A search that fails or never starts is refunded in full.

A bigger cap lets a search read further. It never costs more than the cap, and it stops early when few people
match. "Search my database again" on a search (the database pass) is charged the same way, from that search's
own cap, while the search still holds it; once a search has finished, expand it instead.

A project's searches share a limit of three searches' worth. For a different role or place, start a new project.

## Company searches

A search for companies that are hiring is priced per company, for the data you choose on the review:

| Data | Credits per company | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Companies hiring now | 2 | Every company on the list: its open roles, size, industry and hiring score |
| Hiring signals | 5 more | Senior leaders who left or joined, title moves and a new top leader, with dates |
| Decision makers | 3 more | Who to call, up to 6 named people |

All three come to 10 credits a company. Companies are scored on their open roles first; hiring signals and
decision makers are bought for the top companies your credits cover, and charged only for a company where
some turn up. Companies already in your results, and staffing agencies left out, are free. A list that finds any company is charged at least 100 credits, and the
same prices apply on a trial.

The review shows what your credits cover before you start: "Up to 1,000 credits: up to 40 companies with hiring
signals and decision makers, 10 credits each." When the search finishes, the project page shows where the
credits went, for example "Finding 60 · Hiring signals 150 · Decision makers 90". The same numbers are in
`credits_breakdown.companies` on `GET /v1/searches/{id}`.

## Free trial

A free trial is 500 credits, at plan prices: 3 credits per new profile, the same as on a plan. Each search can
reserve up to what you have left. When the trial is used up, choose a plan to keep searching.

## Your balance

Credits left are under **Settings → Usage**. Credits held by searches still running come back into your balance
as they finish. A plan grants its credits each period; a top-up (**Settings → Plan & billing**) buys more at your
plan's own rate.

Developers: `GET /v1/me/usage` (CLI `revcenter usage`, MCP `get_usage`) returns `credits_remaining` and your
workspace's own limits and price in `credit_terms`. Each search's charge is in `credits_charged`,
`profiles_delivered` and `credits_breakdown` on `GET /v1/searches/{id}`, and for a whole project in
`GET /v1/projects/{id}/spend`.
