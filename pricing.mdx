**3 credits per profile delivered. People you already have are free.**

You pay for one thing: new people. A search is charged for each person it delivers to your workspace for the
first time, whatever their score. Once your workspace has someone, every later search and the candidate database
use them again for free.

## What a shortlist costs

A shortlist costs the new people its searches delivered, not the people on it. A search reads everyone who might
fit and scores them, and the best of them make your shortlist; you pay for each new person read, whether they
made the list or not. People listed but cut before they are read cost nothing.

For example, a search that delivers 81 new people is charged 243 credits (81 × 3), whether 20 or 60 of them
reach the shortlist. Searching the same role again later costs less: people the first search delivered are free
the second time.

Before a search starts, the review shows the most it can use and what that covers: 1,000 credits covers
up to 333 profiles. When it finishes, the project page shows what it was charged and why, for example
"81 profiles × 3 = 243 credits".

## How a search's credit limit works

1. **Held.** When a search starts, its credit limit is held from your credits. The limit is your workspace's limit
   per search unless you name less ("only use 2,500 credits"). On a plan the limit is 5,000 unless your admins set
   another under **Settings → Workspace → Credits per search**; on a trial it is 1,000.
2. **Charged.** When it finishes, it is charged for each new person it delivered, never more than the limit.
3. **Refunded.** The rest of the limit comes back. A search that fails or never starts is refunded in full.

A bigger limit lets a search read further. It never costs more than the limit, and it stops early when few people
match. "Search my database again" on a search (the database pass) is charged the same way, from that search's
own limit, while the search still holds it; once a search has finished, expand it instead.

A project's searches share a limit of three searches' worth. For a different role or place, start a new project.

## Company searches

A search for companies that are hiring is priced per company, for the data you choose on the review (the prices
are in the table above):

- **Companies hiring now:** every company on the list, with its open roles, size, industry and hiring score.
- **Hiring signals:** senior leaders who left or joined, title moves and a new top leader, with dates.
- **Decision makers:** who to call, up to 6 named people.

Companies are scored on their open roles first; hiring signals and decision makers are bought for the top
companies your credits cover, and charged only for a company where some turn up. Companies already in your
results, and staffing agencies left out, are free. The same prices apply on a trial.

The review shows what your credits cover before you start, for example:

> Up to 1,000 credits: up to 100 companies, the top 50 with hiring signals and decision makers.

When the search finishes, the project page shows where the credits went, for example "Finding 60 · Hiring signals 150 · Decision makers 90". The same numbers are
in `credits_breakdown.companies` on `GET /v1/searches/{id}`.

## Free trial

A free trial pays plan prices: the same credits per new person as on a plan. Each search can reserve up to what
you have left. When the trial is used up, choose a plan to keep searching.

## Your balance

Credits left are under **Settings → Usage**. Credits held by searches still running come back into your balance
as they finish. A plan grants its credits each period; a top-up (**Settings → Plan & billing**) buys more at your
plan's own rate.

Developers: `GET /v1/me/usage` (MCP `get_usage`) returns `credits_remaining` and your workspace's own limits and
price in `credit_terms`: `credit_terms.actions` is the table above with your plan's dollars, and
`credit_terms.dollars_per_credit` what one credit costs on your plan (null on a trial). Each search's charge is in
`credits_charged`, `profiles_delivered` and `credits_breakdown` on `GET /v1/searches/{id}`, with
`credits_breakdown.actions` naming each action it was charged for, and for a whole project in
`GET /v1/projects/{id}/spend`.
