Add Revcenter to your chat app and ask for the people you need in your own words. The app drafts the search,
shows it to you, runs it when you say yes, and reads you the shortlist. Nothing to install.

You'll need this address:

```text
https://api.revcenter.ai/mcp
```

## Connect

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Claude">
    1. Open **Customize → Connectors**, click **+ Add** and choose **Add custom connector**. On Team and
       Enterprise, an owner adds it first under **Organization settings → Connectors** (**Add → Custom → Web**),
       then members click **Connect**.
    2. Name it Revcenter, paste the address and click **Continue**, then **Add**.
    3. Click **Connect**, sign in to Revcenter, pick your workspace and click **Authorize**.

    Works in Claude on the web, desktop and mobile.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="ChatGPT">
    1. In ChatGPT on the web, open **Plugins**, click **+** and choose **Add custom MCP server**. On Business,
       Enterprise and Edu, a workspace admin turns on developer mode first (**Workspace settings → Permissions &
       roles**).
    2. Name it Revcenter, paste the address under **Connection** and choose **OAuth**.
    3. Accept the warning, choose **Create as a plugin**, sign in to Revcenter and pick your workspace.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Any member of the workspace, Admin or Editor, can connect, on a trial or a plan. The app acts as you: an Editor's
connection sees only their own projects. Connecting never counts toward your workspace's key limit.

## What you can ask

- "Find me a Plant Controller within 30 miles of Camden, NJ, CPA required." The app drafts the search and shows
  you the plan, including anything to check.
- "Run it, and use up to 2,500 credits." It asks you first, then waits for the search to finish.
- "Who's on the shortlist?", "Why is the list short?", "Shortlist anyone 75 or above."
- "Take Dana off the list", "Don't contact Dana again."
- "Give me the client link", "Email the list to the client."
- "Find more people for this role", "Who do we already have for this in our candidates?"
- "How many credits do I have?"

## What it asks you first

The app always asks before it:

- starts a search or finds more people (it uses credits, and says how many it may use),
- changes the shortlist your client sees,
- emails your client (it shows who gets what).

Reading, drafting and answering questions run without asking. Repeating a launch returns the same search and
never charges twice. Decisions and notes you record on a person are private to your team. Projects are
archived from a chat, never deleted.

Searches take a few minutes. Ask the app to wait for the search, then to list everyone scoring 80 or more.

## Disconnect

**Settings → Integrations → Connectors** lists your connected apps; **Revoke** stops one at once. Connecting the
same app again replaces its earlier connection.

## Security

- You sign in on Revcenter's own page. The app never sees your password.
- The connection works only for the one workspace you picked, and only at `https://api.revcenter.ai/mcp`. It
  renews itself every hour; revoking it stops it at once.
- Everything the app does goes through Revcenter as you: the same permissions, credits and limits as in the app.
