Add Revcenter to Claude or ChatGPT and ask for the people you need in your own words. It drafts the search, shows
it to you, runs it when you say yes, and reads you the Shortlist. Nothing to install.

You'll paste this address when you connect:

```text
https://api.revcenter.ai/mcp
```

## Connect

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Claude">
    1. Open **Customize → Connectors**, click **+ Add** and choose **Add custom connector**. On Team and
       Enterprise, an owner adds it first under **Organization settings → Connectors** (**Add → Custom → Web**),
       then members click **Connect**.
    2. Name it Revcenter, paste the address and click **Continue**, then **Add**.
    3. Click **Connect**, sign in to Revcenter, pick your workspace and click **Authorize**.

    Works in Claude on the web, desktop and mobile.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="ChatGPT">
    1. In ChatGPT on the web, open **Plugins**, click **+** and choose **Add custom MCP server**. On Business,
       Enterprise and Edu, a workspace admin first turns on developer mode under **Workspace settings →
       Permissions & roles**.
    2. Name it Revcenter and paste the address under **Connection**. For sign-in, pick the **OAuth** option: it
       means you sign in with your Revcenter account.
    3. Accept the warning, choose **Create as a plugin**, sign in to Revcenter and pick your workspace.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Anyone in your workspace can connect, Admin or Editor, on a trial or a plan. Claude or ChatGPT then works as you:
an Editor sees only their own projects, just as in the app.

## What you can ask

- "Find me a Plant Controller within 30 miles of Camden, NJ, CPA required." It drafts the search and shows you
  the plan, including anything to check.
- "Run it, and use up to 2,500 credits." It asks you first, then waits for the search to finish.
- "Who's on the Shortlist?", "Why is the list short?", "Shortlist anyone 75 or above."
- "Take Dana off the list", "Don't contact Dana again."
- "Give me the client link", "Email the list to the client."
- "Find more people for this role", "Who do we already have for this in our candidates?"
- "How many credits do I have?"

Searches take a few minutes. Ask it to wait for the search, then to list everyone scoring 80 or more.

## What it checks with you first

Claude or ChatGPT always asks you before it:

- starts a search or looks for more people (it uses credits, and says how many it may use),
- changes the Shortlist your client sees,
- emails your client (it shows who gets what).

Reading, drafting and answering questions happen without asking. Asking twice for the same search never charges
twice. Notes and decisions you record on a person stay private to your team. Projects can be archived from a
chat, never deleted.

To download a spreadsheet, use the Revcenter app.

## Disconnect

Go to **Settings → Integrations → Connectors** to see your connected apps. **Revoke** disconnects one at once.
Connecting the same app again replaces the earlier connection.

## Keeping your account safe

- You sign in on Revcenter's own page. Claude and ChatGPT never see your password.
- The connection only works for the one workspace you picked. Revoking it stops it at once.
- Everything it does goes through Revcenter as you, with the same permissions and credits as in the app.
